# Maddie Journey V4.3.5 — Premium Visual Depth & Interaction System

**Release intent:** Add visual sophistication to executive dashboards without introducing extra KPI blocks, colors, clutter, data entry, additional pages or expensive animations. Implemented as a final progressive presentation layer before V4.4 stabilization.

## What changed

### 1. Editorial layout and surface depth
- Dashboard headers use larger, more deliberate typography and consistent whitespace.
- Data panels have a clear difference between the *primary* item, supporting content, and detailed records.
- Neutral paper surfaces, restrained border contrast and two levels of shadow provide visual depth without decorative gradients or pastel backgrounds.
- The Overview's **Needs attention** panel is the priority surface. **Workforce** is a quieter feature surface; other record panels remain neutral.

### 2. More intentional KPI and chart composition
- KPI values gain stronger typographic weight without changing their calculated values.
- V4.1's two visual insight panels use **asymmetric widths on desktop** (approximately 58/42) and stack on narrower screens.
- The primary chart has a stronger neutral header accent; its companion has a quieter treatment.
- Line plots, bar tracks, labels, source links, and executive observation strips share one cohesive visual grammar.

### 3. Accessible chart inspection
- Recorded values on V4.1 SVG points, bar rows and breakdown legend items can be inspected with a **pointer or keyboard**.
- Each inspectable item is linked to **its existing chart label**, not a new calculation, target or predicted value.
- A compact floating readout appears within the chart panel, **without causing layout shifts**.
- Keyboard focus is visible and the readout announces values through `role=status` and `aria-live=polite`.
- Data charts do not change on hover. Neither source values nor report links are rewritten.

### 4. Profiles and organization structures
- V4.3 employee profile snapshots, timeline and grouped fields gain subtle layering and clearer distinction.
- Organization chart person chips have restrained hover/focus feedback. The connected one-screen layout, all names, all division levels and compact node dimensions remain unchanged.
- No new hover card reveals compensation or confidential details.

### 5. Responsive and accessible motion
- At widths under 1050px, insight panels stack vertically rather than cramping annotations.
- At widths under 690px, visual depth and panel padding are reduced.
- Micro-interactions use short approximately 120–180ms transitions and never delay data display.
- Both `prefers-reduced-motion` and the prototype's `.mj-reduced` setting are respected.
- Only black/white/neutral surfaces are used; original division accent colors remain on existing semantic details.

## Implementation files

- `maddie-v435-depth.css` — final cascade layer after V4.3, strictly scoped to dashboard `#view` and employee modal `#layer`.

- `tests/v435-depth-contract.js` — repeatable dependency-free DOM interaction checks.
- `index.html`, `demo.html` — both load the same versioned CSS and JS assets.
