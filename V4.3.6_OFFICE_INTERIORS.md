# V4.3.6 — Living Virtual Office Interiors

## Scope

Improves the existing interactive Three.js Virtual Office directly. Keeps the original nine room destinations, fictional people counts, read-only monitoring records, room links, camera controls, and light/dark theme handling.

### Room-specific details

- Design: concept/mood board, collaborative table, lounge and shelves.
- Media: editing displays, camera/tripod, studio lighting, equipment and storage.
- Corporate Operations: filing drawers, workflow board and round discussion table.
- Revenue Management: analytics boards, bar charts and review table.
- CEO/COO: executive desk, lounge and coffee setting.
- GenCon: coordination whiteboard and project meeting table.
- Business Development: presentation screens and client conversation table.
- Headquarters: reception, globe and lounge area.

### Staff behavior

Employees cycle through low-amplitude walking, conversation, desk work, meeting, and coffee activities. Coffee mugs lift in both the overview proxy and focused detail scene. The number of figures still derives from existing fictional employee records. A rearranged three-column workstation layout creates dedicated social and collaborative areas without adding invented employees.

### Implementation

- `maddie-office-furnishings.js` contributes batched geometric props without image dependencies.
- `p-hub.js` drives employee activity and keeps instanced overview meshes for efficiency.
- The furnishing kit loads ahead of the hub in `index.html` and `demo.html`.
- Lite quality mode omits tiny decorative props; reduced-motion settings continue suppressing avatar motion.
- No changes to permissions, integrations, navigation routes, data calculations, or personnel datasets.

### Regression

Run `node tests/v436-office-interiors-contract.js`. Also verify manually on desktop/mobile: WebGL overview, each clickable division, focused room, employee selection, theme switch, lite mode, reduced-motion, and CSS fallback when WebGL is unavailable.
