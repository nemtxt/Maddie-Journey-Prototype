# Maddie Journey V4.1 — KPI Visualization & Executive Insights

Status: implemented on `main`, building on the V4.0 design system.

## Scope

This release adds responsive, monochrome, data-derived insight panels. It does **not** replace report content or sources, modify fictional employee records, create write operations, add logins, or affect the black sidebar, passcode screen, organization chart, or 3D Office.

- `maddie-v41-insights.js`: chart and insight generation. Reads the existing `window.MCD` adapter and `MCD.model` data; nothing is hardcoded as a KPI.
- `maddie-v41-insights.css`: final black/white chart and executive-insight presentation. Loaded after V4.0.
- `index.html` and `demo.html`: load identical V4.1 assets.

## Metric provenance and truthful definitions

- **Headcount series:** `MCD.MON` and `MCD.HC`, from the model's historical trend. Difference is an *absolute* change between first and last recorded months; not annualized.
- **Monthly late entries:** `MCD.TL` and `MCD.MON`. Event counts, not unique employees. Division 30-day counts are the sum of `EMP[].l30`.
- **Division headcount and employment status:** `MCD.D` and `MCD.EMP`.
- **201-file completion:** per-employee `EMP[].f` already computed by the existing sample tracker adapter; chart averages are unweighted arithmetic means of employee percentages. Not verified compliance.
- **Journey stages and levels:** `EMP[].stage` and `EMP[].orgLevel` (fallback to `lvl`). Levels are not proof of direct reporting lines.
- **Recruitment:** `MCD.PIPE`, `model.talent.open`, `model.talent.candidates.total`, `model.talent.interviews.total`. No conversion rates or hiring targets inferred.
- **Governance:** `MCD.CASES`. Unknown case-status labels remain **Other** rather than being treated as resolved.
- **Overdue tasks:** `MCD.OPS[division].stats.overdue` when `loaded=true`. Unmapped divisions are excluded and the denominator of mapped divisions is displayed.
- **Priority signals:** `MCD.EXC` severities exactly as provided; not a risk prediction.
- **Learning signal:** training-related event titles matched by a documented keyword rule. Not completed course participation.
- **Compensation coverage:** count of available basic-salary fields only; no actual amounts displayed.
- **Narrative insights:** generated from the same data arrays at page render time, not from fabricated thresholds or targets.

### Verified fictional dataset snapshot (2026-11-20)

51 employees; 8 divisions; headcount trend May–Nov `43,43,45,46,49,51,51`; 84% average 201-file completion; 22 incomplete 201 files; 4 missing IDs; 12 overdue tasks across 7 mapped divisions; monthly lates October 6 and November 19.

These values describe the current **fictional fixture**, not a live People & Culture system.

## Interaction and accessibility contracts

- Charts are native SVG/CSS and use accessible text labels; no chart-library dependencies.
- Each chart includes readable numeric values and context, so color is not the only information carrier.
- Links use valid existing hashes and preserve all original report routes.
- Mobile stacks the two insight cards; reduced-motion preferences disable bar transitions.
- The app observer ignores its own insertions and does not repeatedly rebuild a route's chart.
- The dashboard-only CSS is confined to `#view .mj-v41`; it cannot change the sidebar or office scene.
